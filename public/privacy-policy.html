Introduction

Welcome to FindMyPuppy ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe experience for all users, especially children. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service").

COPPA Compliance: This app is designed to be COPPA-compliant and suitable for children under 13. We take children's privacy seriously and have implemented measures to protect their information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Username: Required to create an account and play the game
  • Email Address: Required for account creation (for users 13+)
  • Password: Encrypted and stored securely (never stored in plain text)

Game Data

  • Game progress (levels completed, scores, hints used)
  • Points and achievements
  • Purchase history (for in-app purchases)
  • Theme preferences

Technical Information

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Usage statistics (game sessions, time played)
  • IP address (for security and analytics purposes)

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To provide game features and functionality
  • To save game progress and sync across devices
  • To process in-app purchases securely
  • To improve our services and fix bugs
  • To send important account notifications (password resets, etc.)
  • To prevent fraud and ensure security
  • We do NOT use personal information for marketing purposes

3. Data Storage and Security

Encryption

All passwords are encrypted using industry-standard bcrypt hashing before storage. Passwords are never stored in plain text and cannot be retrieved or viewed by anyone, including our staff.

Storage

  • Data is stored on secure servers hosted by MongoDB Atlas
  • All data transmission uses SSL/TLS encryption
  • We use secure authentication methods
  • Regular security audits and updates

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

FindMyPuppy is designed to be COPPA-compliant and safe for children under 13.

Parental Consent

  • For users under 13, we require verifiable parental consent before collecting any personal information
  • Parents can review, modify, or delete their child's information at any time
  • Parents can request account deletion by contacting us

Limited Data Collection

  • We only collect minimal information necessary for gameplay
  • No location tracking or geolocation data
  • No social media integration for children under 13
  • No personalized advertising based on personal information

Advertising Guidelines

  • We may show age-appropriate advertising through Google AdSense
  • Ads are not personalized based on personal information for children
  • We do not use behavioral advertising for children under 13
  • Advertising is clearly labeled as such

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

Payment Processing: Razorpay

Payment information is processed securely by Razorpay and never stored by us.

Authentication: Google Sign-In

Optional Google sign-in (users 13+) is handled by Google's secure authentication system.

Advertising: Google AdSense

We may show age-appropriate ads. AdSense may collect anonymous usage data for ad personalization.

Data Storage: MongoDB Atlas

Game data is stored securely on MongoDB's cloud servers with encryption.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

We DO NOT sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers who help us operate the app (payment processors, hosting services) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • If required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • In case of a business transfer (merger, acquisition) with prior notice
  • With your explicit consent

7. Data Retention

  • We retain account information as long as your account is active
  • You can delete your account at any time, and we will delete your personal information within 30 days
  • Some information may be retained for legal or security purposes as required by law
  • Game statistics and scores may be retained in anonymized form for analytics

8. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Modify: Update or correct your account information
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Opt-out: Disable certain features or data collection
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address provided below.

9. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

We comply with applicable data protection laws, including:

  • GDPR (EU): European users have rights including access, rectification, erasure, and data portability
  • CCPA (California): California residents have rights to know, delete, and opt-out of sale of personal information
  • COPPA (US): Special protections for children under 13 with parental consent requirements

If you are in the EU, UK, or California, you have additional rights. Please contact us to exercise them.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your rights, or need to contact us regarding your child's account, please reach out:

Developer:

Email:

findmypuppys@gmail.com

For parental consent requests, account deletions, or privacy concerns, please include your username and account information in your email.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Posting a notice in the app
  • Sending an email notification for material changes

Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Find My Puppy - COPPA Compliant Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025
Effective Date: January 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Find My Puppy! We are committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of your children. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App"). This policy is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

⚠️ Important Notice for Parents: This App is designed for children under 13 years of age. We take children's privacy very seriously and comply with COPPA requirements. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect the following information:

  • Email Address: Used for account identification and communication
  • Password: Securely encrypted and stored (we cannot see your actual password)
  • Username: A display name you choose for your account
  • Google Account Information (if using Google Sign-In): Email, name, and profile picture (only if you choose to sign in with Google)

2.2 Game Progress Data

We collect the following game-related information to provide and improve our services:

  • Level progress (levels completed in Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulties)
  • Game scores and points earned
  • Hints purchased and used
  • Premium subscription status
  • Purchase history (for in-app purchases)

2.3 Technical Information

We automatically collect certain technical information to ensure the App functions properly:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version and usage statistics
  • Last activity timestamp (to track online status)
  • Error logs and crash reports (to fix bugs)

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect:

  • Real names or physical addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Location data (GPS coordinates)
  • Photos or videos from your device
  • Contact lists or address books
  • Biometric data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: To create and manage your account, authenticate logins, and provide customer support
  • Game Functionality: To save your game progress, track scores, and unlock levels
  • Service Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, and enhance the gaming experience
  • Communication: To send important updates about the App, security notifications, and respond to your inquiries
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Encryption

We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data:

  • Password Encryption: All passwords are hashed using bcrypt (one-way encryption) before storage. We cannot see or retrieve your actual password.
  • Data Transmission: All data transmitted between your device and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Database Security: User data is stored in MongoDB Atlas with encryption at rest

4.2 Storage Location

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by MongoDB Atlas, which may be located in the United States or other countries. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data regardless of storage location.

4.3 Security Measures

We implement multiple layers of security to protect your information:

  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls limiting who can view user data
  • Monitoring for unauthorized access attempts
  • Secure authentication protocols

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

5.1 We Do NOT Sell Your Data

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting it.

5.2 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may have access to certain information:

  • Google Authentication: If you choose to sign in with Google, Google processes your authentication. We only receive your email, name, and profile picture. Google's privacy policy applies to their services.
  • Razorpay (Payment Processing): For in-app purchases, payment information is processed by Razorpay. We do not store your credit card or payment details. Razorpay's privacy policy applies to payment transactions.
  • Google AdSense (Advertising): We may display advertisements through Google AdSense. These ads may use cookies or similar technologies. Google's privacy policy applies to advertising services.
  • MongoDB Atlas (Database Hosting): Our database is hosted on MongoDB Atlas, which provides secure cloud storage. MongoDB's privacy policy applies to their hosting services.

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government regulation, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

🔒 COPPA Compliance: This App is designed for children under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

6.1 Parental Consent

If a child under 13 wishes to create an account, we require verifiable parental consent before collecting any personal information. Parents can:

  • Review the information collected from their child
  • Request deletion of their child's account and data
  • Refuse to allow further collection or use of their child's information

6.2 Limited Data Collection for Children

For children under 13, we only collect the minimum information necessary to provide the App's functionality:

  • Email address (with parental consent)
  • Username (not their real name)
  • Game progress data

6.3 Parental Rights

Parents have the right to:

  • Review their child's personal information
  • Delete their child's account and data
  • Revoke consent and refuse further collection
  • Request a copy of their child's data

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or regulatory purposes.

8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account at any time by contacting us at the email address provided below. Upon account deletion:

  • Your account information will be permanently deleted
  • Your game progress will be erased
  • Your purchase history will be anonymized (for legal/accounting purposes)

8.2 Access and Correction

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request a copy of your data

8.3 GDPR Rights (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under GDPR:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification (correction)
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

8.4 CCPA Rights (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected
  • Know if your personal information is sold or disclosed
  • Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell your data)
  • Access your personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

9. Advertising

Our App may display advertisements through Google AdSense. These advertisements:

  • Are appropriate for children (we use COPPA-compliant ad networks)
  • Do not collect personal information from children under 13
  • May use cookies or similar technologies (with appropriate safeguards)

You can opt-out of personalized advertising through your device settings or by contacting us.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
