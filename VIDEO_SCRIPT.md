# FindMyPuppy - How to Play Video Script

## Video Overview
**Duration:** 2-3 minutes  
**Style:** Fast-paced, engaging, with clear visual demonstrations  
**Target Audience:** New users who want to learn the game quickly

---

## Scene 1: Introduction (0:00 - 0:15)
**Visual:**
- Game logo appears with animation
- Show the home screen with difficulty selection
- Quick montage of beautiful AI-generated scenes

**Narration:**
> "Welcome to FindMyPuppy! An exciting hidden object game where you rescue adorable puppies hiding in AI-generated magical worlds. Let's learn how to play!"

---

## Scene 2: Starting a Game (0:15 - 0:30)
**Visual:**
- Show difficulty selection (Easy, Medium, Hard)
- Select "Easy" mode
- Show level selection screen
- Tap on Level 1
- Show loading screen with progress bar

**Narration:**
> "First, choose your difficulty: Easy for beginners, Medium for a challenge, or Hard for experts. Then select a level. Each level features a unique AI-generated scene with hidden puppies!"

---

## Scene 3: Game Interface Overview (0:30 - 0:45)
**Visual:**
- Show the game screen with all UI elements
- Highlight each element with animated circles:
  - Top-left: Attempts counter (3 puppy icons)
  - Top-right: Puppies found counter (X / Total)
  - Top-center: Timer (if applicable)
  - Bottom-right: Hint button

**Narration:**
> "Here's your game interface. Top-left shows your remaining attempts - you have 3 wrong guesses before game over. Top-right shows how many puppies you've found. And the hint button is always available when you need help!"

---

## Scene 4: Basic Controls - Panning (0:45 - 1:00)
**Visual:**
- Show the large game image
- Demonstrate dragging left, right, up, and down
- Show arrows indicating direction
- Zoom out to show the full image is much larger than screen

**Narration:**
> "The game image is huge! Drag your finger or mouse to explore. Look left, right, up, and down - puppies can be hiding anywhere in the scene. Don't just look at the center - explore every corner!"

---

## Scene 5: Zooming (1:00 - 1:15)
**Visual:**
- Show pinch-to-zoom gesture (or Ctrl+Scroll on desktop)
- Zoom in on a suspicious area
- Show a tiny puppy that becomes visible when zoomed in
- Zoom out again

**Narration:**
> "Pinch to zoom in for a closer look, or zoom out to see the bigger picture. Puppies can be very small, especially in Hard mode, so zooming is essential!"

---

## Scene 6: Finding Puppies (1:15 - 1:35)
**Visual:**
- Show a puppy hidden in the scene (slightly visible)
- Tap on the puppy
- Show the celebration animation (puppy bounces, scales up)
- Show the counter updating
- Find 2-3 more puppies in quick succession

**Narration:**
> "When you spot a puppy, tap or click on it! Found puppies will celebrate with a bounce animation. Keep finding them until you've rescued them all!"

---

## Scene 7: Wrong Attempts Warning (1:35 - 1:50)
**Visual:**
- Show tapping on empty space (no puppy)
- Show attempts counter decreasing (3 → 2 → 1)
- Show warning animation when down to 1 attempt
- Demonstrate what happens on 3rd wrong attempt (game over screen)

**Narration:**
> "Be careful! You only have 3 wrong attempts. If you tap 3 places where no puppy is hiding, the game ends. Watch your attempts counter in the top-left corner!"

---

## Scene 8: Using Hints (1:50 - 2:10)
**Visual:**
- Show hint button pulsing
- Tap the hint button
- Show 1-2 puppies getting highlighted with golden glow
- Show camera auto-scrolling to highlighted puppies
- Show hint counter decreasing

**Narration:**
> "Stuck? Use a hint! Tap the lightbulb button to reveal 1-2 puppies. The game will automatically scroll to show you where they are. You get 2 free hints per level!"

---

## Scene 9: Completing a Level (2:10 - 2:25)
**Visual:**
- Show finding the last puppy

- Show points being awarded

**Narration:**

**Visual:**

## Production Notes

### Visual Style:
- Use screen recording of actual gameplay
- Add animated arrows/indicators to highlight UI elements
- Use zoom/pan effects to show important details
- Add text overlays for key points
- Use smooth transitions between scenes

### Audio:
- Upbeat, friendly background music
- Clear, enthusiastic narration
- Sound effects from the game (puppy found sound, hint sound, etc.)

### Editing Tips:
- Keep cuts fast-paced (2-3 seconds per action)
- Use slow-motion for important moments (finding puppies)
- Add zoom-ins on UI elements when explaining
- Use color highlights to draw attention
- Add progress indicators for multi-step processes

### Tools Recommended:
- **Screen Recording:** OBS Studio, QuickTime (Mac), or ScreenRec
- **Editing:** Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or CapCut (free)
- **Graphics:** Canva, Figma, or Adobe After Effects for animations
- **Voiceover:** Use text-to-speech (ElevenLabs, Murf) or record your own

### Alternative: Animated GIF Guide
If video production is challenging, I can help create a series of animated GIFs showing each step, which can be embedded in the app or website.
